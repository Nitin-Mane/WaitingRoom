# WaitingRoom Complete User Guide & Feature Index

## 1. Navigating WaitingRoom

### Top Navigation Bar & Global Search
- **Command Palette (`Ctrl+K`):** Global instant search for items, contacts, and quick navigation.
- **Focus Flow:** One-click entry into the full-screen kinetic triage flow.
- **Theme Switcher (`palette` icon):** Cycles across **Dark Navy**, **Midnight OLED**, **Cyberpunk Slate**, and **Light Clean** themes.
- **Notification Bell:** Instant access to the real-time Notification Center.
- **User Avatar:** Opens profile settings and account controls.

---

## 2. Core Views & Workflows

### 1. Overview Dashboard
- **Kinetic Risk Radar Gauge:** Real-time animated circular gauge indicating average blocker impact.
- **Category Velocity Spectrum:** Frequency distribution across recruiter, academic, and technical categories.
- **14-Day Calendar Timeline Matrix:** Chronological visual matrix of response deadlines with glowing 'Today' markers.
- **Action Required & Highest Impact Queue:** Direct triage shortcuts.

### 2. Waiting Items Directory
- Dense, structured list view with multi-faceted filtering (Status, Category, Sort by Impact/Due Date/Age).
- Batch operations and search bar.

### 3. Kinetic Focus Mode
- Distraction-free single-item triage flow.
- Big counterparty metadata, factor scores, and one-click actions (*Mark Responded*, *Snooze 3d*, *Escalate*, *Draft Follow-up*).

### 4. Follow-Up Message Composer
- Auto-generates structured outreach templates (*Gentle Nudge*, *Formal Escalation*, *Meeting Follow-up*, *Document Receipt*).
- Tone controls: *Polite*, *Firm*, *Executive*, *Casual*.
- Copy to clipboard or launch default email client with auto-recorded audit trail event.

### 5. Notification Center
- Grouped feeds: *Action Required*, *Updates & Resolutions*, *Upcoming Windows*, and *System Health*.

### 6. Contacts & Directory
- Counterparty profiles, contact methods, responsiveness metrics, and active dependency links.

### 7. Schedule, Deadlines & SLA
- Chronological timeline showing overdue, approaching, and scheduled response windows.

### 8. Analytics & Reports
- Velocity charts, resolution time trends, and counterparty responsiveness rankings.

### 9. Activity History & Audit Trail
- Tamper-evident chronological timeline of every transition, communication, score recalculation, and edit.

### 10. Data Import & Export Wizard
- JSON full backup snapshot, CSV spreadsheet export/import, and Markdown summary generators.

### 11. Application & Profile Settings
- 8 comprehensive tabs: Profile & Account, General Preferences, Appearance, Cadence, Notifications, Privacy, Backup, and API.
